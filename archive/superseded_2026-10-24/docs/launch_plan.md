# Dehydrated fruit business launch from Poranki

Start with **orange wheels and lemon wheels**, sold as garnishes to nearby beverage-focused cafés and bars. Offer lime as a separately specified variant, then test pineapple with buyers who have a suitable drink. Choose the first production range from paid trials and reorders before committing to a machine capacity or large inventory.

The fruit order below is a recommended trial order, **not measured local demand**. Public menus reveal possible applications, but do not reveal how much a venue buys, whether it dries fruit in-house, or whether it wants a new supplier. Lush Bengaluru's published Highlander cocktail specifically includes dehydrated orange, providing one concrete regional example. [Lush menu](https://www.lushbengaluru.com/our-menus)

## Fruits to test by business type

| Buyer | First offer | Next trial | What to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cocktail bars and breweries with cocktails | Orange, then lemon or lime wheels | Pineapple for a suitable tropical drink | Current garnish process, diameter, appearance, bitterness and acceptable cost per usable piece |
| Mocktail and iced-tea cafés | Lemon and orange wheels | Pineapple | Which named drink will use it and whether a paid garnish improves the drink |
| Bakeries and patisseries | Orange for decoration | Apple, pineapple or mango pieces for a specific recipe | Texture, piece size, ingredient specification and recipe performance |
| Breakfast-bowl and snack cafés | Buyer-led apple or mango trial | Banana pieces if requested | Desired chew or crunch, portion size and willingness to pay |
| Hotels | Citrus samples for the bar; separate chef discussion for bakery | Approved recipe-specific fruits | Supplier onboarding, documentation, purchasing cycle and payment terms |
| Basic bars without a cocktail menu | Qualify first | None until a use is identified | Alcohol service alone does not establish demand for garnishes |

Mirth's fruit-based drinks, Pandhal's granola bowl and Fresh Choice's bakery/beverage range support these trial ideas. They do not prove demand for the dehydrated form. [Mirth](https://www.mirthblr.com/), [Pandhal](https://www.pandhal.in/cafe-deli), [Fresh Choice](https://freshchoice.in/)

Dried citrus garnish does not replace fresh juice. Lemon and lime are separate products; confirm the variety with the buyer. Hot-air-dried banana or mango may be chewy, so demonstrate the actual texture instead of promising fried-chip or freeze-dried crunch. [Montana State drying guidance](https://extension-store.montana.edu/montguides/drying-fruits)

## Local selling order

Begin with Poranki, Tadigadapa and Kanuru, then make a Vijayawada route around qualified cafés and breweries. The workbook sorts these ahead of Hyderabad. Use **Local First** for calls and visits and **Leads** for source links and caveats. P1 means an early outreach priority, not a confirmed buyer.

Call first and ask for the owner, beverage lead, head bartender or pastry chef. For hotels, ask F&B to route you to procurement. Published restaurant reservation and general-support contacts are introductions; they are not automatically purchasing contacts. If an email is missing, request a business inbox by phone instead of guessing it.

Hyderabad and other southern cities are second-stage prospects. Start shipping only after checking breakage, moisture protection and freight economics. Coordinate shared purchasing groups, including Zero40 and Room Two, before sending duplicate pitches.

## Four week pilot

These activity targets are a suggested working plan, not a sales forecast.

For initial samples, consider borrowing or renting suitable equipment, arranging a supervised vendor demonstration, or using an appropriate food-production partner. Establish the same food-safety and batch controls for these samples. This lets you test the product before choosing a larger machine; it does not require promising orders you cannot yet supply.

1. **Week 1:** Call or visit 10 nearby prospects. Identify at least one actual drink or food application at each interested venue. Record their present supplier or in-house method, preferred fruit and willingness to test.
2. **Week 2:** Arrange five small tastings with qualified buyers. A proposed trial kit is 10 orange wheels and 10 lemon wheels, individually identified by fruit and batch. Agree timing and any sample charge first; do not dispatch unrequested samples.
3. **Week 3:** Seek two paid trial orders. Agree size, usable piece count, net weight, delivery, storage instructions and price. Record acceptances and rejects in the workbook.
4. **Week 4:** Ask for a reorder and review actual batch costs. Expand only the fruits with repeat use. If buyers decline, find out whether the obstacle is price, appearance, freshness, texture or lack of a useful application.

Ask: “Which drink would use this?”, “How many of that drink sell in a week?”, “Do you already buy or dry garnish?”, “What price per usable wheel works?”, and “Who approves a trial order?” Keep estimated weekly usage blank until the buyer supplies it.

## Before buying a machine

Ask the seller to run your chosen fruit and slice thickness. Compare adjustable temperature control, airflow, timer, cleanable food-contact trays, usable tray area, service support and replacement parts. A fan and horizontal airflow can help tray consistency; published tray count alone does not establish usable finished output. [University of Georgia equipment guidance](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/drying-general/food-dehydrators/)

Measure the demonstration: wet input, usable dry output, number of saleable pieces, rejects, elapsed time, labor and electricity. Test pieces from different trays. Ask for the food-contact material specification, warranty, cleaning procedure and service availability in Andhra Pradesh. Compare quotes only after these details are known. This pack does not endorse a machine model or quote current machine prices.

Use the **Pilot Costing** worksheet with actual measurements. Account for fruit, power, labor, packaging, delivery, testing and overhead. Quote citrus by usable piece count and net weight; a kilogram price alone can hide differences in wheel size and breakage. A paid trial and reorder provide better capacity evidence than a venue's total footfall.

## Product readiness

Establish a repeatable preparation and drying process, batch identification and traceability. Validate the product, packaging, storage conditions and shelf life with an appropriate food laboratory or food technologist before making commercial shelf-life claims. Weight loss and moisture content alone do not establish water activity. [FDA water activity explanation](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/inspection-technical-guides/water-activity-aw-foods)

Cool the product appropriately before packing and test moisture-resistant food-contact packaging. Small service packs can reduce repeated opening in humid conditions. Household storage guidance is not a commercial shelf-life study. [University of Georgia storage guidance](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/general-information-drying/packaging-and-storing-dried-foods/)

Use the current official FoSCoS process for the applicable food-business registration or licence and verify product and labelling requirements for your activity. FSSAI revised its turnover bands from 1 April 2026; avoid relying on older articles quoting the previous threshold. The March 2026 order sets registration up to ₹1.5 crore, state licensing above ₹1.5 crore up to ₹50 crore, and central licensing above ₹50 crore. Confirm your classification and current requirements in the portal. [FSSAI order](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69b4054bb6cd6Order%20dated%2013thMarch2026_Revised%20Turnover%20threshold.pdf), [FSSAI FAQ](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69c6a23234827order_27032026.pdf), [FoSCoS](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/)

Only state claims such as “no added sugar,” “preservative-free,” or a shelf-life duration when the actual ingredients, process and supporting evidence justify them. Replace all placeholders in outreach drafts with confirmed details.
