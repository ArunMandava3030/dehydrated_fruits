# Dehydrated fruit outreach drafts

Use these drafts after confirming the business is operating and the recipient handles ingredients or beverages. Replace bracketed fields before sending. These messages introduce a proposed business honestly; they do not imply existing customers, completed licensing, proven shelf life or guaranteed savings. No emails have been sent.
